HOW TO USE

Look at the reference. Make your own comparison.

  1. Inspect the full image and matching detail.
  2. Rank the four candidates for the selected criterion, with the best first.
  3. Use “Tie” for indistinguishable adjacent results, or “Cannot judge”.
  4. Save each judgment. Switch samples or criteria to revisit your choices.

Boundary: contour location and transition fidelity. Halo: absent bright/dark fringes or overshoot. Texture: preservation of real fine detail.

All images use unchanged brightness and contrast. Zoom is synchronized. Candidate order is shuffled for each sample in your browser session. Crops are preselected demonstration examples.
